Fine fleece Huacayas from the hills east of Braidwood

Breeding stock, stud services and friendly wethers, from an established Huacaya stud with a long show record.

750 metres up, off the Kings Highway

On many afternoons a sea breeze carries low cloud over the escarpment and it settles across the paddocks and the forest. Locals call it the Monga Mist. It’s cool, wet country, and good for growing fleece.

For breeders

Bob Kingwell
