A few terms

For readers new to fleece testing.

FD
Average fibre diameter of a fleece sample, in microns (µ).
SD
Standard deviation of fibre diameters: how much the diameters vary either side of the FD.
CV
Coefficient of variation: the SD as a percentage of the FD.
CF
Comfort factor: the percentage of fibres finer than 30 microns.
CEM
Coarse edge micron: the number of microns between the FD and the coarsest 5% of fibres.
SUM
Score of Uniform Micron, Bob’s measure: FD + SD.
Primaries and secondaries
The two kinds of fibre in an alpaca fleece. The coarser primaries become guard hair as an alpaca ages.
